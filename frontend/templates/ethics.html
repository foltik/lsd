Ethics/Accountability Committee

A completely external board. It advises L&SD's role-leads on ethical issues, community standards, and accountabilities at large.

The Role

Purpose: keep L&SD's events safe, maintain clear community standards of conduct, and ensure that all accountabilities are being met.

  • Internally: Serve as a sounding board for all role-holders on any and all ethical/accountability questions or issues, and provide feedback to the circle via regular meetings.
  • Externally: All suggestions/concerns/feedback L&SD receives from its anonymous feedback form are reviewed by the committee and provided to the circle for discussion and deliberation.

First projects will include developing the studio's disciplinary guidelines, code of conduct, and mediation process.

Who can run

You may put your own name forward if you do not currently fill any role at L&SD.

How seats are filled

The committee will hold 7 seats, and will be elected by the circle's role-leads. The secretary opens the call by email to all@ and a post at the studio. The call stays open.

  1. Eligible candidates nominate themselves to the committee via the form below. The deadline for the first round of nominations is 10/15/2026, but the call will remain open thereafter on a rolling basis.
  2. Election Process: Each core member ranks up to seven names on that list. Rankings are written and revealed together.
  3. The facilitator proposes the seven names with the most points, and breaks a tie. If fewer than seven people ran, those people are the proposal and the remaining seats stay empty.
  4. The circle objects or not, one name at a time. A valid objection drops that name. The next person in ranking order moves up.
  5. Once seven members have been ranked, the secretary asks each candidate to confirm their seat. A no, or no reply, drops them, and the next name moves up. A yes fills the seat.

Term Limits

A person may serve two six-month terms, then sits out a term. If you are out for a period and have an alternate step in, the alternate's time does not count towards your term limit.

At the end of each six-month term, the role holders will hold another election. Any committee members that want to remain for a second term will let the committee know before the election.

Anyone can leave the committee at any time.

Expectations

There may be urgent issues that require the committee to meet more frequently. In that case, the committee will meet as needed.

The committee will otherwise meet once a month. If necessary, the committee will hold a call or in-person meeting with the role-leads to discuss any issues or concerns.

The committee will self-govern, and will be able to propose changes to the scope of its responsibilities via L&SD's governance process.

Self-nominate
