01 — Open

A studio for making worlds.

Light & Sound Design Studio is the container for a decade-long practice of building environments, shaping atmosphere, and creating experiences that make another world feel possible, tangible and within reach.

L&SD’s Luxury Service Division works to make those possibilities accessible to our clients and their communities.

02 — Lineage

It started with a gathering.

The Last Saturday Dance began in 2016. There was no venue, production department or playbook. Just a room, a sound system, some lights and the instinct to transform the space, creating unique worlds for a community to enter with intention.

Over the years, the gathering moved through factories, warehouses, studios, metal shops and borrowed rooms. Every time, the environment had to be built. Then broken down. Then built again. That repetition became a practice.

In 2022, we found a permanent container for it: a loft on Huron Street.

03 — The Incubator

The loft became an incubator for the practice.

Ideas get tested here at full scale. Light, sound, design, fabrication, food and atmosphere come together in one environment, in real time, with real people.

Build. Observe. Refine. Repeat. A decade of gathering has become a way of working: experimental, collaborative and deeply hands‑on.

The party was never the point. The practice was.

04 — The Practice

The room is an instrument, its character shaped through light, sound and design.

Light

We design it, program it and live with it. Light changes the scale of a room, directs attention and shapes how people move through it. It is not decoration. It is part of the architecture of the experience.

Sound

We built the room around deep listening. The system is part of the architecture, not something added after the fact. The sound is physical, immersive and considered from every position in the room.

Over the years, the room has held Francois K, DJ True, Jacky Sommer, UMADJ, livwutang, Ron Like Hell, Enzo, Takaya Nagase, Mo Yasin, Niyah West, Stonie Blue, datadata, Scott Zacharias, Toribio, 30,000AD, Ayanna Heaven, Genevieve D, Mark Ernestus, Ron Trent, Keith Fullerton Whitman, HxH, A Space for Sound, CCL, Objekt, SLINK, KWata, DJ Marcelle and KG.

Artists come to contribute to the room's history, not simply to play in it.

Design

The environment is the work. Architecture, atmosphere, objects, light and sound are considered together, then shaped through use. Nothing is fixed. The room evolves through what happens inside it.

05 — Fabrication

If the environment needs to be built, we build it.

For years, the practice has extended beyond the room itself into custom environments, fixtures, structures and objects. The same hands that build the studio build what the experience requires. Custom lighting. Custom furniture. Custom structures. Built for the space, the moment and the people inside it.

That work has taken shape for Nike, Maison Margiela, American Eagle, Vogue, Harper’s Bazaar, Calvin Klein, Pyer Moss, Max Mara and others across events, editorial and brand experiences.

At Huron, fabrication is part of the practice. The speakers, the booth, the walls, the objects around you: built here, tested here, refined here. If it needs to exist, we can make it.

06 — Nourishment

Nourishment is integral to the worlds we create.

Chefs and cooks are part of the practice, creating food that belongs to the room rather than simply arriving in it. There are no catering menus. We construct food around how people gather and what engages their senses.

The same kitchen that has fed this incubator can become part of yours, thoughtfully integrated into your experience.

07 — The Living Element

Atmosphere takes form through natural materials.

We introduce flowers, foliage and organic materials into the architecture to alter the character of the space and create tension between what is built and what is alive. These materials bring color, texture, scale and movement into the room.

This work is responsive to the space and to the intentions we are setting. It grows from the environment rather than sitting on top of it.

Our floral artists work with locally sourced materials to make that transformation possible, bringing their own sensibility to the room and to the worlds we create.

08 — One Practice

Creative direction, production and fabrication are part of the same practice.

We can develop the idea, shape the environment, build what it requires and produce the experience from beginning to end. One studio. One team. One shared point of view.

The result is a world that feels considered from every angle because it was made that way.

09 — Close

Bring Us the Brief. We’ll build the world around it.

We want to work alongside you to give physical form to your vision, creating experiences that are considered, immersive and enduring.
